About Nepalese Football Team Weingarten

“United by passion, driven by teamwork, inspired by our heritage.”

Who We Are

The Nepalese Football Team Weingarten (NFT Weingarten) is a group of passionate Nepali youth living in Germany, brought together by our shared love for football and teamwork. Founded with the goal of promoting unity and healthy competition within the Nepali community, NFT Weingarten has grown into a strong and inspiring team representing our hometown and heritage across different tournaments in Germany.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a positive environment that encourages Nepali youth to engage in sports, teamwork, and discipline. Through football, we aim to connect our culture with our community abroad — fostering friendship, fitness, and national pride wherever we play.

Our Journey

From small friendly matches in Weingarten to participating in national tournaments like the NRNA Germany Cup and Red Bull Cup, NFT Weingarten has built a reputation for sportsmanship and determination. Each season brings new challenges, victories, and lessons — and every player contributes to our growing legacy both on and off the field.

Our Values

  • Teamwork: We believe unity creates strength.
  • Respect: For the game, the opponents, and our supporters.
  • Dedication: We train, play, and represent with full heart.
  • Community: Giving back through friendship and solidarity.

Future Goals

NFT Weingarten is growing fast. Our goal is to strengthen the team both technically and mentally, and to create a sustainable platform for upcoming Nepali players in Germany. We aim to host local events, friendly matches, and youth training sessions — inspiring future generations to chase their dreams with the same passion.
