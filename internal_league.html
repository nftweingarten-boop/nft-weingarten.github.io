📅 1st September 2024 – Senior vs Juniors

The first-ever “Senior vs Juniors” match, held on September 1, 2024, was a thrilling encounter filled with laughter, competitive energy, and great sportsmanship. The Seniors showcased their experience and composure, while the Juniors impressed with their energy and quick play. After an exciting contest, the Juniors clinched a narrow 1–0 victory, but the true win was the bond formed across generations.

📅 20th February 2025 – Senior vs Juniors

The 2025 edition was even more intense — the Juniors came prepared to defend their pride! The weather was perfect, the energy unmatched, and both sides gave everything on the field. After a thrilling 1–1 draw in regular time, the Juniors triumphed once again, winning 4–3 on penalties. It was another exciting and friendly internal showdown that strengthened team spirit across generations.
